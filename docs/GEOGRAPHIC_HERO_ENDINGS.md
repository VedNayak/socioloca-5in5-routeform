# Geographic hero endings

Internal page destinations now use a spherical Fibonacci distribution with geographic UVs, sampled against the same local land mask as Home. Ocean dots are heavily attenuated, rear-facing dots fade, and a small seeded fraction carries orange. A separate geographic point layer emerges while the transient assembly particle field converges and fades. Existing diagnostics `data-particle-opacity` describe that transient field, not the finished globe.

Services: three illustrative flight connections with original fuselage/wing/tail silhouettes moving along the same curved geometry used by the routes. Each aircraft orientation derives from its route tangent and radial up vector. About: transatlantic and Indian Ocean connections sampled through explicit sea waypoints. Industries: two restrained trade connections. Quote: one connection. These are demonstration routes, not fictional company capabilities or locations.

All destinations reuse the existing per-page camera framing, keeping the finished globe between mobile copy and chapter controls. The land-mask texture is disposed with the renderer. Home renderer and its accepted transition are unchanged.

Explicit scroll instructions, hero progress rail and workflow stage counts guide pinned narratives. Reduced motion shows static views and manual-stage instructions. Existing chapter controls and pause buttons remain available.
